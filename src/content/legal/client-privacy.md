At Diamond Shine Professional Cleaning Service, trust is the foundation of our work. We are invited into homes, family spaces, and private properties, and we treat that invitation with the respect it deserves.

## Respect for clients and their homes

We respect our clients' privacy, families, personal belongings, schedules, addresses, and the private spaces of every home we enter. Our team is trained to be present, professional, and mindful at all times.

## Discretion from our team

Every member of the Diamond Shine team is expected to treat client information, property access, photos, and service details with discretion. We do not share client names, addresses, schedules, or personal details casually or publicly.

## Use of photos and videos

We may capture photos or short videos during service for the following purposes:

- Preparing estimates and quotes
- Internal quality assurance and training
- Documenting conditions before and after service
- Occasional marketing of our work, when appropriate

When photos or videos are used publicly, we avoid showing private or sensitive details, such as identifying addresses, personal documents, family photos, valuables, or anything that could compromise a client's privacy. If you prefer that no images of your property be used for marketing, simply let us know.

## Talk to us

If you have specific privacy preferences or sensitivities, for any reason, we welcome the conversation. Reach us at [hello@diamondshinefamily.com](mailto:hello@diamondshinefamily.com).
