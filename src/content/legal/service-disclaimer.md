At Diamond Shine, we pride ourselves on careful, high-quality work. To keep expectations clear and protect every client, please review the following disclaimer before your service.

## Recurring care and schedule changes

Your recurring rate is tied to the approved rooms, condition, and visit interval: weekly (every 7 days), biweekly (every 14 days), or monthly (one agreed visit each calendar month). Cleaning is billed per completed visit unless your written agreement expressly states otherwise.

### Skipped, delayed, or extended visits

If you skip, delay, or extend your agreed interval, the next visit is reassessed. Additional charges apply when accumulated buildup or changed scope requires extra work. We confirm the revised scope and price in writing before service; the original recurring rate is not automatically carried forward.

No extra work or catch-up charge proceeds without your approval. You may approve the revised visit, agree a smaller scope, or decline before work begins. Any cancellation, late-change, or access fee must have its amount and notice period stated in your accepted service agreement; there is no automatic per-day charge.

A longer pause or changed property condition may require a separately quoted deep reset before recurring pricing resumes. Permanent frequency changes receive a new recurring quote. Preferred slots are subject to availability when a plan resumes.

A reschedule initiated by Diamond Shine does not trigger a client schedule-change or accumulated-days surcharge. Existing accepted bookings keep their agreed terms unless a change is mutually approved.

For example, moving a weekly visit from day 7 to day 14 requires review of the next visit's scope and price. We may apply your approved biweekly rate or quote additional catch-up work. We do not charge twice for the same added work.

## Cleaning scope and welcome touches

Our work centers on cleaning, restoration, and preservation of the home. Washing, drying, ironing and clothing care are not offered. Active White Glove Society members receive bed making and fresh-linen changes for the beds identified in their written offer during each separately paid residential cleaning visit; the client supplies ready-to-use clean linens and used linens are placed in the designated basket.

Essential Refresh maintains the approved kitchens, bathrooms, floors, reachable surfaces, and room presentation after the initial reset. Baseboard and ceiling-fan detailing, interior appliances, specialist restoration, and other deep-detail work are separately scoped unless explicitly included in your written plan.

Bottled water and individually wrapped mints are included as welcome touches with residential cleaning visits. Tell us if you prefer us to leave them out.

On request, we may include a complimentary wipe of safely accessible washer and dryer surfaces and cleaning of a removable lint screen or other manufacturer-approved accessible filter. Suitability and scope are confirmed first. This does not include moving appliances, dismantling equipment, internal dryer vents or ducts, repairs, or replacement filters.

Additional hospitality arrangements and outside-provider work require their own approved scope and charges.

## Kitchen and appliance care: scope and results

Routine stovetop and exterior surface cleaning follows your selected service and written scope. These requests identify interior cleaning or additional attention to buildup; they do not remove work already included.

Oven and refrigerator interior detailing is performed only when included in your written scope. We review each unit, its condition and accessible areas before confirming the work and price.

We aim for the best improvement that is safe for your appliance. Complete stain or buildup removal and a like-new finish are not guaranteed. Permanent stains, discoloration, scratches, corrosion, worn coatings and heat damage may remain. Cleaning does not repair damage. If further treatment could harm the surface, we will pause and discuss the limits with you.

We do not promise a fixed percentage of stain, odor or buildup removal. Results vary with material, age, prior care and existing condition. This limitation concerns the result of cleaning; it does not excuse careless work or damage caused by our team.

Range-hood detailing covers agreed accessible surfaces and manufacturer-removable washable filters only. It does not include ducts, fan or motor disassembly, repairs, or a fire-safety inspection. We follow the appliance manufacturer's care instructions and do not force removal of components.

Before service, please disclose known damage or special care instructions. We will confirm whether the oven needs to be cool and how refrigerator contents should be handled. We do not discard food, move connected appliances or expand the agreed work without authorization.

We document visible concerns, explain what may remain and obtain approval for any revised scope or price before proceeding. Your service agreement records the agreed appliances, quantities, condition and limitations; submitting a quote request is not a signed service agreement.

Laundry service is not offered: no washing, drying, folding or ironing. Laundry-room surfaces are only included when listed in your agreed cleaning scope.

## Pre-existing conditions

Diamond Shine is not responsible for pre-existing damage or conditions, including but not limited to:

- Old, set-in, or permanent stains and discoloration
- Worn, faded, scratched, or aged surfaces
- Improper prior treatments, sealants, or DIY repairs
- Weak, cracked, or missing grout and caulking
- Damaged, warped, or deteriorating flooring
- Delicate, antique, or specialty materials
- Items, conditions, or sensitivities not disclosed before service

## Results and limitations

Our restoration and deep-cleaning services are designed to significantly improve appearance and condition. However, we cannot guarantee complete removal of every stain, odor, paint mark, buildup, or form of damage. Outcomes depend on materials, age, prior care, and the severity of the condition.

We will always communicate honestly about what we expect to achieve before starting work.

## Client responsibilities

To ensure a safe and successful service, clients are responsible for:

- Providing safe, lawful access to the property
- Working water, electricity, and (when needed) air conditioning
- Removing or securing valuables, cash, jewelry, and fragile items
- Securing or relocating pets during service
- Disclosing known hazards, allergies, or sensitive materials in advance
