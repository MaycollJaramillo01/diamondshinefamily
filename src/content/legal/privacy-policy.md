Diamond Shine Professional Cleaning Service ("Diamond Shine," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website, request a quote, or receive service from us.

## Information we may collect

- Name, phone number, and email address
- Service address and details about the property
- Photos or videos of service areas, when you choose to share them
- Quote details, requested services, and scope notes
- Payment-related information processed through trusted providers
- Communication history (messages, emails, and call notes)

## How we use your information

We use the information you provide only to:

- Prepare quotes and estimates
- Schedule and deliver service
- Communicate with you about your project
- Process payments securely
- Improve our services and customer experience
- Protect our team, our clients, and our business

## We do not sell your information

Diamond Shine does not sell client personal information. Your details are handled with discretion and only shared with trusted service providers when necessary to run our business, for example payment processors, scheduling tools, website hosting, or communication platforms.

## Children's privacy

Our website and services are directed to a general audience. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
