The Good Samaritan · A community mission founded by Sergio and Lilian Leal.

Diamond Shine proudly supports The Good Samaritan, a nonprofit organization founded by Sergio and Lilian Leal. Through deliveries of food, household essentials, clothing, and toys, the organization helps adults and children in need in Mexico.

## A shared commitment to giving back

Donations from members of the Diamond Shine family help support this mission. Their generosity contributes to practical essentials and thoughtful gifts for people who need them.

## Looking ahead

The family hopes to expand The Good Samaritan's community support into the United States over time, building thoughtfully on the work already underway in Mexico.

Diamond Shine extends its heartfelt thanks to everyone who has contributed and become part of this mission. Each contribution is part of a shared commitment to giving back.
